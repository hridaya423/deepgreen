Deep Green: a neural network that plays optimal Wordle

Deep Green is a 1,550,400-parameter neural network (6.2 MB) that plays Wordle decision for decision like a certified optimal solver. It sees only the public board: past guesses and their colors. It runs in your browser. Enter any of 5,022 words to watch it solve, in normal or hard mode.

In the release proof it matched the solver on every position checked, with zero losses in both modes.
